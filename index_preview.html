Rules & Guidelines
✓
SANDLOCK USER POLICY

Safe. Fair. Reliable.

Please follow these rules for a smooth beach-locker experience.

01
One user — one locker

Each active account may reserve one locker at a time.

02
Your locker, your responsibility

Use only the SandLock assigned to your reservation.

03
Respect your time

Use the locker only during the selected reservation window.

04
Keep your PIN private

Do not share your temporary reservation PIN.

05
15-minute reminder

You receive a notification 15 minutes before your reservation ends when notifications are permitted.

06
Late fees apply

Any overtime is charged at 3× the locker's normal hourly rate until you end the session.

07
Payment & fees

Pricing is calculated from your selected duration and locker rate.

08
Damage policy

You are responsible for confirmed damage. Repair or replacement costs may be charged to your account.

09
Prohibited items

Do not store illegal, hazardous, living, or odor-producing items.

10
Internet & device access

Remote unlock requires an active app connection. Your reservation PIN remains the backup user-access method.

11
Remote unlock

Use mobile unlock only for your own active reservation.

12
Cancellation policy

Cancellation terms and refund rules are defined by the beach operator.

13
Misuse consequences

Fraud, tampering, or repeated misuse may result in account suspension.

14
Safety & security

Always close the door after use and verify the locker is secured.

15
Need help?

Use Help & Support from your profile if you experience a problem.

🛡
Thank you for respecting SandLock

Help & Support
◉
WE'RE HERE TO HELP

What can we help with?

Quick answers for common SandLock issues, plus a prototype support request form.

My mobile unlock is not working

Check your active reservation and internet connection, then use your reservation PIN on the keypad as the user backup method.

I forgot my reservation PIN

Open My Reservations → Live Status to view the PIN linked to your active booking.

The locker appears offline

Do not repeatedly force the lock. Use the support request below and include the locker ID.

The door will not close

Check that no belongings obstruct the door, close it gently, and verify the live door status.
